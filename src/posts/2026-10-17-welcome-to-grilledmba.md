Hi, and welcome to the very first issue of **GrilledMba**.

This is a space for one simple habit: taking ideas from the MBA classroom and from the headlines, and putting them over the fire. What actually happened? Why does it matter for a business? Which framework explains it — and where does the framework break?

## What's on the menu

- **Articles** — deeper pieces on global markets and the fields an MBA covers: finance, marketing, strategy, operations, HR and economics.
- **The Newsletter** — regular issues, each built around one theme.
- **Life & Lessons** — personal experiences from the MBA journey: case competitions, interviews, classrooms and everything in between.
- **Videos** — short clips and quick takes.

## The house rules

1. Facts come with sources. Opinions and estimates are labelled as such.
2. Nothing here is investment advice.
3. Clear over clever. If a concept can be explained simply, it will be.
